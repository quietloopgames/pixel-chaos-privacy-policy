Pixel Chaos
Privacy Policy

iOS and Android mobile game · Last updated October 11, 2026

Pixel Chaos is a mobile game by Quiet Loop Games. This policy applies to the game on iOS and Android and explains how the game and its service providers handle information. Platform-specific services are described below.

Game progress stored on your device

The game saves your progress and preferences on your device, including scores, Pop Coins, unlocked heroes and items, achievements, Supporter Pack status, and audio and camera settings. Quiet Loop Games does not operate a game account or its own cloud save server for Pixel Chaos. Deleting the app may erase local progress; platform purchase records and leaderboard entries are managed separately by the relevant platform provider.

Optional platform leaderboards and achievements

If you choose to sign in to a platform gaming service, Pixel Chaos submits your cumulative number of circles popped and earned achievements to Apple Game Center on iOS or Google Play Games on Android. The platform provider handles your player account and profile. Other players may see leaderboard information according to your platform profile settings. You can play without signing in.

Advertising and privacy choices

The free game may show a menu banner, an ad after eligible runs, and an optional rewarded ad if you choose to revive. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process your IP address (which can indicate general location), device or app identifiers where permitted, device and app information, ad views and interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. The information processed depends on your region, device settings, and consent choices. Where required, the game presents an advertising consent form and an in-app Privacy Choices control. The optional Supporter Pack removes banner and end-of-run ads and provides one ad-free revive per run.

Optional purchase

The Supporter Pack is a one-time in-app purchase. Apple processes purchases on iOS and Google processes purchases on Android. The game checks your purchase entitlement to unlock or restore its benefits. Quiet Loop Games does not receive your full payment card or bank details from those stores.

Information you send us

If you email us for support or a privacy request, we receive the email address and information you choose to include. We use it to reply and resolve the request, and retain the correspondence only as reasonably needed for that purpose or legal obligations. Please do not send sensitive personal information in a support message.

Permissions and service providers

Core gameplay does not request access to contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location. Internet access may be used for platform leaderboards, in-app purchases, advertising, and consent services. These providers handle information under their own policies:

  • Apple Privacy Policy (App Store and Game Center on iOS)
  • Google Privacy Policy (AdMob and Google Play services on Android)

Your choices and data requests

You can play without a platform leaderboard sign-in, decline an optional rewarded ad, manage advertising consent through Privacy Choices where offered, and remove locally stored progress by deleting the app. Platform records such as purchases and leaderboard entries are subject to Apple or Google account controls. For access, correction, deletion, or other privacy requests concerning information held by Quiet Loop Games, contact us below. We will respond as required by applicable law.

Children and changes

Pixel Chaos has no chat feature and does not ask players to provide their name or contact details to play. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, please contact us. We may revise this policy if the game or third-party services change; the update date above will change when we do.
